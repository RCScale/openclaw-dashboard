Goal 2 Phase A — reviewer distributions

Reviewer-level distributions used to pick outlier thresholds. Outliers are sampled into Phase B for LLM audit.

What we measured

For every reviewer with ≥5 reviews we plot the distribution of their mean QM score, mean feedback length, and review volume. Outlier thresholds in these distributions decide who gets sampled for the Phase B audit.

What we found

53 reviewers passed the volume floor; 30 flagged as outliers; 200 reviews sampled for audit.
