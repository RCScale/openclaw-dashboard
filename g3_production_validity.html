Goal 3 — Phase D: production validity

Per-question difficulty vs PDR-based discrimination, plus the internal-vs-production comparison scatter.

What we measured

Phase B measured course-internal discrimination: did getting this question right correlate with getting OTHER course questions right? This panel substitutes the y-axis with production validity from Goal 4 step 2: did getting this question right correlate with the worker's PDR downstream? We plot −r_pdr so UP = predictive (matches Phase B).

What we found

Internally-consistent questions can still be production-counterproductive. The lower-right quadrant of the second scatter (high internal r, negative production r) lists questions selecting for the wrong trait — the highest priority rewrite candidates.

What to do
