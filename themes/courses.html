What we measured

For each course we count how many of its questions are predictive (passing them is linked to fewer downstream defects) vs counterproductive (passing is linked to more defects). We also look at curriculum gaps: mistakes that show up in bad reviews but aren't well covered by any course.

Behind the numbers

Predictive / counterproductive: see the Questions theme page for the per-question definition. This page aggregates those flags up to course level.

Curriculum gap: a mistake cluster (mined by LLM from bad-review feedback) with total_coverage_score ≤ 2 (weakly addressed by any course) AND n_records_matched ≥ 20 (frequent enough to matter).

Sources: analysis/g4_question_predictive.csv, analysis/g4_mistake_coverage.csv, analysis/g4_mistake_clusters.csv from analysis.run_g4 step-2 + analysis.run_g4_mistakes.

For 'courses covered but workers still fail' analysis (35 pairs found), see g4 Step 5.

Things to watch for
  • The 'predictive / counterproductive' aggregate hides effect-size variation — a course with 1 strong predictive + 2 weak counterproductive questions reads as net-negative, but the strong question may dominate in practice.
  • Curriculum-gap coverage is scored by an LLM reading section titles + a sample of question stems. A course that thoroughly teaches a topic in its body but doesn't name it in a section title may be undercounted.
  • Mistake-cluster names come from a single LLM pass over the bad-review feedback corpus. Verify against the example task_attempt_ids in g4_mistake_clusters.csv before acting on a cluster.
