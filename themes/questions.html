Behind the numbers

For each (course, question) we compute a point-biserial correlation between whether a worker passed the question and that worker's PDR (Performance Defect Rate = fraction of their last 3 trusted reviews scoring below QM=4, the promotion gate). Lower PDR = better. A NEGATIVE r_pdr means passers have a lower defect rate downstream.

  • Predictive: r_pdr < −0.05.
  • Counterproductive: r_pdr > +0.05.
  • No clear signal: |r_pdr| < 0.05.

Source: analysis/g4_question_predictive.csv + analysis/g4_question_bodies.json. Built by analysis.run_g4 step-2.

For full diagnostic depth on any single question (including LLM-driven diagnosis of why it correlates the way it does), see g4.html Step 2.7.

Things to watch for
  • Effect sizes are small (|r_pdr| typically ≤ 0.20). PDR is bounded to {0, 0.33, 0.67, 1.0} for a 3-review window, which deflates correlation magnitudes.
  • A counterproductive flag could mean (a) the answer key is wrong; (b) the question selects for an off-axis trait; (c) the LLM grader rewards the wrong feature on free-text. Diagnose before acting — see Step 2.7 for the tag-based cross-validation.
  • Questions with sparse text bodies (no body snippet shown) live in nested Q-G2 sections our walker did not reach. The correlation is computed correctly; we just can't display the prompt inline.
