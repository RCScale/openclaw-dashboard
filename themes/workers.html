Overview/Workers

Workers

Who is performing, who is stuck, who is failing? Sorted so the triage queue is at the top.

What we measured

We measure each worker on PDR (Performance Defect Rate) over their last 3 trusted reviews. PDR=0 means every recent review was above the promotion gate (QM ≥ 4); PDR=1 means none of them were. We also track the pipeline funnel.

What we found

495 workers with recent trusted reviews. Mean PDR is 72% — most workers fail more reviews than they pass. 210 are stuck >30 days in pipeline.

What to do

  • Triage the 210 stuck workers below.
  • Look at the deep-dive g4 dashboard to see which traits + tags predict lower defect rate — the top tag ('reviewer_on_multiple_projects') drops PDR from 80% to 44%.
  • For workers with PDR=1, decide: more training, reassignment, or pipeline exit.

Behind the numbers

PDR (Performance Defect Rate) = fraction of a worker's last 3 trusted reviews where quality_overall_qms < 4 (below the promotion gate). Range [0, 1]. Trusted reviews exclude any from reviewers dropped in step 1.

Stuck = days_in_stage > 30 from analysis/g1_stuck_workers_initial.csv, ranked desc.

For per-trait + per-tag analysis (which characteristics best predict lower PDR), see Step 6 of the g4 dashboard.

Things to watch for
  • PDR with a 3-review window can only take values {0, 0.33, 0.67, 1}. Workers with fewer than 3 trusted reviews use however many are available.
  • 'Stuck' is calendar-based; a worker who completed their last attempt 30 days ago is flagged even if they're still active in another project.
  • Most workers in this cohort have PDR=1.0. That collapses the metric — use mean_qm_in_window from g4_worker_pdr.json for finer comparisons.
