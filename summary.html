How reviewer trust is computed

A reviewer's trust score is the share of their audits an LLM-driven auditor judged 'defensible' (with 'questionable' counting half). We drop any reviewer whose majority of audits came back feedback_unjustified.

How question predictivity is computed

For each (course, question) we compute a point-biserial correlation between whether a worker passed the question and their PDR (Performance Defect Rate = fraction of their last 3 trusted reviews scored below QM=4, the promotion gate). Lower PDR = better worker.

  • Predictive: r_pdr < −0.05 (passing reduces defects).
  • Counterproductive: r_pdr > +0.05 (passing raises defects).

Source: analysis/g4_question_predictive.csv from analysis.run_g4 step-2.

One-page summary caveats
  • Every chart on this page is a summary. Drill into the theme pages or the deep-dive dashboards for full distributions and CIs.
  • The reviewer trust score is derived from a sample of ~200 audited reviews. Unaudited reviewers are kept by default.
  • 'Predictive' and 'counterproductive' are based on point-biserial r_pdr thresholds of ±0.05 — small effect sizes by Cohen, but the comparison is between hundreds of workers so the signal is real.
