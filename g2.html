Goal 2 — reviewer feedback trust

Per-reviewer trust score from LLM audit of claims in their feedback. Drives the trusted-review pool used in Goal 4.

What we measured

We sampled reviews from each reviewer and had an LLM auditor judge whether each claim in their feedback was defensible (supported by the rubric + the worker's output), questionable, or unjustified. Trust = share defensible (with questionable counting half).

What we found

40 reviewers audited; baseline trust (control mean) = 0.28. The trust-vs-volume scatter is the canonical view: outliers below the baseline are candidates to drop.

What to do

  • For per-reviewer evidence cards, open the audit evidence viewer (local-only).
  • For the actionable drop list and downstream impact, see the Reviewers theme page.
